# Android preview: scanning privacy and third-party terms

The distributed wallet APK includes Google ML Kit barcode scanning17.3.0. Its native scanner
is subject to the [ML Kit terms](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms), not the project's
Apache licence. The matching third-party notices accompany the APK distribution.

Google states that ML Kit processes input images on the device and does not send those inputs
or recognition outputs to Google. The SDK sends performance and usage metrics to Google and
may contact Google for updates or hardware-compatibility information. Google's
[privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) describes its processing. See the
[official terms and privacy information](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms) before
installing this preview. This statement concerns ML Kit; it is not a claim that all wallet
network traffic stays on the device.

This is a direct-download experimental preview, not an app-store release or a claim of complete
physical acceptance. Remaining phone checks were operator-deferred. Preserve your existing
wallet store and hardware identity; do not uninstall or clear data to force compatibility.
